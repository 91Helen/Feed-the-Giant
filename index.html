🍎 Feed the Giant! 🍌

Listen to the hungry giant, choose the right food and feed him!

❓ How to play

  1. Press the big button at the bottom to talk to the giant.
  2. Listen carefully. What does the giant want?
  3. Drag the right food into his mouth and say "Here you are!"
  4. Watch the food go into his belly. Every right choice gives you ⭐ points!
